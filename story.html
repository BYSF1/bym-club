01 — WHERE IT BEGAN

THE STORY SO FAR

Every place I haven't been yet lives somewhere in the back of my mind — a skyline caught in a photograph, a coastline I've only heard described, a language whose sound I can't yet place. BYM began as a private wish, whispered mostly to myself on ordinary nights, until it grew too large to keep quiet.

This isn't a business plan, and it doesn't come with a deadline. It's a promise I'm making to the person I was before I stopped believing this was possible — and an open door for anyone who wants to help me walk back through it.

There is a particular kind of fear in saying a dream out loud — the fear that naming it makes the disappointment realer too, if it never comes true. I chose that fear anyway. Better to reach for something in public and fall short, than to stay quietly, safely, exactly where I already was.

02 — THE ROAD AHEAD

MOST OF THE WORLD, SLOWLY

This was never meant to be one trip. It's a promise to reach every continent — the medinas of Marrakech at dusk, the temple bells of Kyoto at dawn, the fjords of Patagonia, the souks of Istanbul, the deserts I haven't even learned the names of yet.

Meeting people and tribes who see the world entirely differently than I do. Tasting food I can't yet pronounce. Sitting with customs that aren't mine, and listening to stories that rarely travel this far.

Wherever I go, I want to stand in the museums and monuments that hold each place's memory — not rushing past them for a photo, but staying long enough to understand why they mattered enough to keep.

One world. Countless stories. And somewhere in all of them, maybe, a little of yours too.

03 — A PROMISE IN RETURN

NOT JUST FOOTPRINTS

For every new country, helping at least one person, in some real way, is part of the plan — not an afterthought. If this journey asks generosity of others, the least I can do is carry some forward myself, everywhere I go.

Not charity performed for an audience — the quiet kind, the kind no one needs to hear about for it to have mattered.

08 — GOOD TO KNOW

A FEW HONEST ANSWERS

Where does the money actually go?

Toward making the journey possible — flights, stays, and, from day one, helping at least one person in every country visited. Once $500,000 is reached, a full public breakdown begins.

Is this a registered charity?

No. This is personal support for an individual journey, not a donation to a registered nonprofit — think of it as backing a person, not an organization.

Why Patreon, and not a direct bank transfer?

Patreon handles secure international payments so supporters anywhere can contribute safely, in their own currency, without this page ever touching anyone's card details.

What if $10,000,000 is never reached?

The journey doesn't wait for that number. It begins in stages as support comes in — $10M is the long-term vision, not a precondition for starting.

Is anything guaranteed in return?

No fixed rewards are promised. This is generosity, not a transaction — though updates and unexpected moments along the way are very much part of it.
