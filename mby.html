THE DREAM BEHIND BYM

NOT A CLUB. A CITY.

MBY is not a sports club, and it isn't a stadium with a season attached. It's the reason the full $10,000,000 matters: the blueprint for an entirely new city, built around competition, culture, art, tourism, and community — active all year, not just on match days.

This is the dream every BYM journey is ultimately walking toward.

A CITY, NOT A STADIUM

MBY CITY

  • Sport & training

    Pitches, pools, tracks, and venues built for major competition.

  • Residence

    Housing for members and teams, and the services a real neighborhood needs.

  • Culture & arts

    Theatres, galleries, and studios for creative production.

  • Entertainment

    Restaurants, cafes, and gathering spaces for everyday life.

  • Tourism

    Hotels and visitor facilities, drawing guests year-round.

  • Community

    A place built to live, compete, create, and gather.

THE PRESIDENT & THE PARLIAMENT

The champion becomes MBY's President for one year — leading, representing, and sharing responsibility for the year ahead, but never ruling absolutely.

Their team inherits the season's leading role. A Parliament made up of representatives from all ten teams balances the President's role, discussing the city's affairs and safeguarding every team's interests.

EXCEPTIONAL, BY DESIGN

THE HOME OF BYM

MBY isn't just a destination for the world — it's the permanent home of everyone who was part of BYM from the start. Every founding supporter carries a standing place within these walls, not as a visitor paying an entry fee, but as someone this city already belongs to a little.

The journey funds the dream. The dream becomes a home for the people who funded it.

FROM MOROCCO

MOROCCO → THE WORLD

MBY is born in Morocco, proudly so — but its ambition was never local. A rival here is never an enemy; a rival is part of what pushes a person to train harder, think deeper, create more, and go further than they would have gone alone.

“Don't just be better than everyone else.
Become a better version of yourself — together.”
